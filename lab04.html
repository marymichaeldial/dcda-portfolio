Lab 4: Retail Fashion Analytics

DCDA 40833 | Mary-Michael Dial

Interactive Business Dashboard

Analysis & Business Insights

Data Preparation
I chose a 2025 reflection and analysis of retail fashion sales for my dataset. Uploading it to Tableau Public required a few steps beforehand to ensure the data computed correctly. The first upload of the CSV misidentified the “Order Date” rather than a date format in Kaggle. This required a manual change in order for it to have the correct chronological timeline. It also made the “discounts” as a decimal, so I changed it to a percentage for readability.
Design Choices
I used three primary metrics for the visualization portion. Brand markdown, percentage return, and reasons were the three prioritized categories in a Grouped Bar Chart format. This showed the relationships between sales and profits across brands. Brand is placed on the Y-axis, while X-axis depicts the markdowns and return rates. Color encoding presents the reasons for return, which allows the viewer qualitative and quantitative representation. The percentage return metric shows a clear relationship between the two.
Expected Paterns
My visualization confirmed the outwear and accessories showed the strongest seasonal trends, which I initially had predicted. I expected higher-priced items to have a larger profit margin, showing a steeper slope in terms of profit from other items.
Surprises
I think the biggest surprise in outliers was the footwear category in the midwest region, which had a disproportionately low profit. Another surprise was the random spike in formal wear sales in July, a month typically considered a dead zone.
Questions for Further Investigation
Are high shipping prices in specific states causing underperformance in profit of heavier items? How does aggressive discounting affect profit margins?
Tool Evaluation
Tableau Public is both a powerful and complex visualization tool, but still has its frustrations. Their abilities in Geospatial Mapping is far superior than any other tool I have used, and accompanied with its interactivity abilities is incredibly helpful. The main limitation was Tableau’s requirements in data being published to their cloud.
